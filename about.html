A small studio, run one WhatsApp chat at a time

MANAS WEBDESIGNS is based in Betnoti, Odisha, and builds websites for the shops, clinics, and small businesses around it. No long forms, no waiting rooms — just a chat, a plan, and a website.

What we do

We design and build websites — from a single page introducing a shop, to a fuller multi-page site with a gallery and contact details. Every site is built to load quickly and to look right on a phone, since that's how most visitors will find it.

Because everything is coordinated over WhatsApp, there's no separate login, ticketing system, or app to install. You send us what you need, we send back drafts, and once you're happy, the site goes live.

How a project runs

The same four steps apply whether you choose the Normal, Moderate, or Premium service.

1

You reach out

Message us on WhatsApp with your business details and which service fits your budget.

2

We plan the pages

We confirm what pages, photos, and information will go on the site before building anything.

3

We put the site together and send drafts for you to review, right in the same chat.

4

Once you approve the final version, the site goes live and you can start sharing the link.
